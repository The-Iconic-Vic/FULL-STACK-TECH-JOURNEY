## 📝 Background Tasks vs Alternatives

| Feature | BackgroundTasks | Celery | APScheduler |
|---------|-----------------|--------|-------------|
| Persistence | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Retry | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Monitoring | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ❌ Yes |
| Scheduling | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Complexity | Simple | Complex | Moderate |
